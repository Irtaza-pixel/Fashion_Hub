The World of CSS

Chapter 1

Ali had been exploring web design for several months now. On his desk, lying between the guides of HTML Basics, Web Design Weekly, and all the other development books he had studied, was a well thumbed copy of Mastering CSS. It had been Ali's favorite subject since university, and the more he practiced styling web pages the more he felt inspired to create beautiful designs.

He had spent the last few years improving his skills, sitting under a poster with a famous quote proclaiming that Design is not just what it looks like, but how it works. Although many considered his ability to style layouts, colors, and fonts as an impressive achievement, Ali did believe he had much more to learn in the world of CSS, especially in understanding the power of CSS.
