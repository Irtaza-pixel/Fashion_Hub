Badminton Basics

Badminton is a fast-paced and fun sport. Here is a guide to get started with playing badminton.

Equipment

  • 1 badminton racket
  • Shuttlecocks
  • Comfortable sports shoes

Rules

  1. Start by serving the shuttlecock diagonally to the opponent's side
  2. Players take turns hitting the shuttlecock over the net
  3. The shuttlecock must not touch the ground on your side
  4. Score a point when the opponent fails to return the shuttlecock
  5. Games are usually played to 21 points, with a two-point lead required to win
